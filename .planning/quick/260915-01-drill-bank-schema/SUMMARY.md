## Why the backfill is excluded

FLE-8: *"Blocked on Task 1's disposition — if the eval says REVISE, this would be a
bank of bad drills."*

`04.1-05-EVAL-RESULTS.md` records **DISPOSITION: REVISE** — 25/30 gates, L5 ordering
failing 3/5 songs, L1 isolation failing on the barre drill. The wake comment that
re-opened this issue cleared a *different* blocker (FLE-3 pushing `main`); it did not
address this one.

`04.1-06-PROMPT-PATCH.md` was applied to `breakdown.py` during this session, but that
does not unblock the backfill, for a reason independent of whether the patch works:
**every breakdown already cached in the DB was generated by the pre-patch prompt.**
Backfilling reads those rows. A patched prompt only affects breakdowns generated
*after* it. So the backfill would bank exactly the drills the eval graded as
defective, no matter how good the new prompt is.

The backfill becomes safe when either the confirming eval re-run passes (still
unpaid — needs a spend decision) **and** affected breakdowns are regenerated, or a
quality filter is agreed for which cached drills are worth banking.

Schema, dedup and history are quality-independent — a table shape is not affected by
whether the rows it will hold are pedagogically good — which is why they shipped now.
