- Pass `call_id` to the wrapped fn via a contextvar (`_current_call_id: ContextVar[UUID]`). The wrapped fn imports `record_estimate` from `app.ai.governor` and calls it immediately BEFORE its `client.messages.create(...)` invocation, once it has constructed the `messages` list.
